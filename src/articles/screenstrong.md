If you read the description, yes it is true. If you did not read the description, I will reiterate: I am cutting video games out of my life for a year.

Video games were a mainstay of my childhood. While other kids were off at summer camp, I was slaying demons in Diablo 2, Lord of Destruction.

And while other adolescents were learning piano, reading books, or socializing, I was leveling up my mage in World of Warcraft.

On the bright side, I think video games can be an enlightening activity, especially if you don't fit it in or your friend circle is into drugs.

However, video games tend to be an immense time suck, eating away valuable hours and leaving you stressed out from competitive play, like the somewhat new fad E-Sports.

I also think video games resemble Soma, the drug from A Brave New World. They lull us into a dull, happy state of impotence.

#Truth Be Told#

One of the nagging reasons I want to give up video games, is because I am no longer that good.

I was never a pro, but I used to be quite good.

Nowadays, however, I don't have the time or desire to be good.

Plus, as the years tack on, I realize I need to move on to adulthood.

Please feel free to reach out about my progress. You can find my email through my Portfolio, which is linked to at the top.

I look forward to reading, socializing, working on my my house, and much more.

All the best!
